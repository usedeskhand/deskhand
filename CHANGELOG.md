# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-25

First public version.

- Plain-English reader: scheduled buys, buy/sell rules (moves, levels, averages, highs and lows, RSI, take profit, stop loss, holding time) and baskets with target weights.
- 30-day replay on hourly on-chain prices, next to holding, with costs and largest drop.
- Paper mode and wallet mode (requests, route check, exact allowances, receipts read on chain).
- Marketplace: free and paid listings in USDG, versions, installs with the buyer's own budget.
- MCP server with ten read-only tools.
- 22 markets on Robinhood Chain: 20 stock tokens, BTC and ETH.
- Public leaderboard, live decision log, markets page with movers and 7-day ranges.
